# Brief: Kopi tubruk

## The idea in one sentence

The bean is the constant from cherry to cup. Tubruk skips the filter, so the whole ground bean goes into the glass with the water, and the wait for the grounds to sink is the turn before the sip.

## Format and spec

- Format: F2 morph chain, because the plot engine is a chronology (a real process in its real order). Each world closes to one shape that becomes the next world. Proven in this grammar (FORMATS.md, F2: a 60s cut-paper history in `examples/history-of-ai`).
- Size: 1080x1920 (main, 9:16) and 1080x1080 (1:1), 24fps, 120 BPM, 20s = 480 frames.
- Style: `cut-paper` (set in `piece.json`). Hero: none separate. The coffee bean (an oval with an S-crease) is the constant: cherry, bean, grounds, and the sip all keep its shape and colour identity. Colour rule: brown-and-red is the bean and the cherry; no other colour is spent on the bean.
- Story checklist deviation to know about: cut-paper's frame checklist asks for a character with a face. The brief says no separate character, so the bean gets no face. I'll flag this at the look check if it reads as a gap.

## Sound plan

- Music only, composed in code (no track, no assets, no voice).
- The silence is shot 6 (16.8-19.0s), a dip of about 2s on a held image, before the payoff. The loudest hit is the sip at 19.0s.
- The busiest moment is the pour (shot 5), not the sip. The sip is the loudest.
- The score grows from thin (cherry) to fullest (pour), then drops out for the wait.

## Claims and sources (web-checked)

- Kopi tubruk is an unfiltered Indonesian coffee. Ground coffee goes into a glass, boiling water is poured over it, and the grounds are left to sink before drinking. Wikipedia, "Kopi tubruk" (https://Www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kopi_tubruk); Nescafé Indonesia guide (https://nescafe.com/id/en-id/coffee-culture/coffee-lifestyle/craving-kopi-tubruk).
- Grind size is disputed in the sources (some say fine, some coarse). The piece says "ground whole" and shows grounds, with no grind-size claim. Jakarta Post, 2008 (https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2008/05/10/enjoying-local-039mud-coffee039).
- Coffee cherries turn red as they ripen, with variety-dependent exceptions (yellow or orange). Coffee Review (https://www.coffeereview.com/picking-ripe-not-as-easy-as-it-sounds/); PMC11243843 for the five colour stages (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11243843/).
- Sangrai (roasting coffee) is done in a wok, and the wok's sloped sides keep the beans moving. Source is a home-roasting blog (https://eathealthy365.com/a-guide-to-the-sangrai-coffee-recipe/). Partial: the English search did not return a detailed account of village sangrai. Treat as likely, not confirmed. The piece shows a wok and stirring, not a specific roast time or temperature.
- Sun drying is shown as a generic picture. No specific claim is made about it.
- Not claimed: "first", "oldest", "best", or any brand. Water temperature is not stated on screen (sources disagree; one blog gives 90-95 C, not corroborated).
